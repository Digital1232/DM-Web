# Sneha's Quick Start - Today's Work Content Types

## What's New?

You now have a quick way to track what types of content you worked on each day. It takes just 10 seconds at the end of your shift!

## Where to Find It

1. Open the **Tasks Hub** (click the checkbox icon in the sidebar)
2. Click the **"Today's Completed"** tab at the top
3. You'll see the new section right at the top:

```
┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
┃ ✓ What did you work on today?              ┃
┃ Select all that apply to track your work   ┃
┃                                            ┃
┃ VIDEO CONTENT                              ┃
┃ ☐ Video Content  ☐ End Card               ┃
┃ ☐ Thumbnail      ☐ Captions              ┃
┃                                            ┃
┃ POSTER CONTENT                             ┃
┃ ☐ Poster Content ☐ Captions              ┃
┃ ☐ Quality Check                           ┃
┃                                            ┃
┃ [Update Work Summary] [Clear Selection]    ┃
┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛
```

## 3-Step Process

### Step 1: Check Your Work Types
Check the boxes for each type of content you worked on today.

**Examples:**
- If you edited videos: Check "Video Content"
- If you made thumbnails: Check "Thumbnail"
- If you did quality checks: Check "Quality Check"
- If you created graphics: Check "Poster Content"

You can check **multiple types** if you did different work!

### Step 2: Review Your Selection
Your selections appear as badges below:

```
[Video Content] [Thumbnail] [Quality Check]
```

Want to remove one? Click the **×** on any badge to unselect it.

### Step 3: Save
Click **"Update Work Summary"** button.

You'll see:
```
✓ Work content types saved successfully!
```

And a summary appears above your completed tasks:
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TODAY'S WORK SUMMARY                   │
│ [Video Content] [Thumbnail] [QC]      │ ×
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Done! Your work is tracked.

## Key Features You'll Love

### 🔄 Auto-Remembered
- Your selections are saved automatically
- Next time you check "Today's Completed", your previous selections are still there
- Perfect if you work on the same types most days

### 📱 Works Everywhere
- Your phone, tablet, laptop - all supported
- Works in light mode and dark mode
- Takes just a few seconds

### 🎯 Date-Specific
- This tracking only appears for **TODAY**
- When viewing yesterday's or past week's work, the selection section disappears
- Keeps things clean and organized

### ✔️ No Pressure
- Totally optional - use it if you find it helpful
- Doesn't affect your existing task tracking
- Just helps your manager see what you're working on

## Tips for Success

### Tip 1: End of Day Check
Spend 30 seconds before leaving to update your content types. Makes it super easy!

### Tip 2: Be Accurate
Check **everything** you worked on, even if just for 15 minutes:
- Did you touch any video? ✓ Video Content
- Did you create any graphics? ✓ Poster Content
- Did you review anything? ✓ Quality Check

### Tip 3: Pattern Recognition
Notice what you usually check and why. This helps your manager assign work better!

### Tip 4: Multiple Types
Don't be shy about checking multiple types. Most days involve variety!

## Need Help?

### "How do I clear my selections?"
Click **"Clear Selection"** button. This removes all checkmarks without saving.

### "I forgot to save, will it disappear?"
- If you close the tab **without clicking Save**, it won't be saved
- If you refresh the page, your checks will stay until you click Save
- Once you click Save, it's permanently stored

### "Can I change what I worked on tomorrow?"
Yes! Each day is separate. Tomorrow's selections won't affect today's.

### "What if I selected the wrong thing?"
Just uncheck it by clicking the **×** on the badge, then click Save again.
