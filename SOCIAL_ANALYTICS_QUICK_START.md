## 📈 Understanding Your Metrics

### KPI Cards (Top Section)

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Total Posts** | Number of posts tracked in selected period |
| **Total Views** | Sum of all views across all posts |
| **Total Likes** | Sum of all likes across all posts |
| **Total Shares** | Sum of all shares across all posts |
| **Followers Gained** | New followers gained from these posts |

### Top Performing Post

Shows the post with the **most views** in your selected period with:
- Post title and description
- Platform and post type badges
- All metrics (views, likes, shares, comments, followers)
- Direct link to the post
- Any notes you added

### Platform Breakdown Chart

Doughnut chart showing:
- How many posts on each platform
- Color-coded by platform
- Click legend items to see breakdown

**Example:**
```
Facebook:   12 posts
Instagram:  18 posts
YouTube:     5 posts
Total:      35 posts
```

### Trend Charts

Two line charts showing performance over time:

**Views Trend**
- Shows how your total views changed day-by-day
- Blue line = views
- Helps spot high-performing periods

**Engagement Trend**
- Shows combined likes + shares + comments over time
- Pink line = engagement
- Helps identify most engaging content types

## ✏️ Editing & Deleting Entries

### Edit Entry
1. Find the entry in the "All Entries" table
2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) on the right
3. Modal opens with current data
4. Make changes
5. Click "Save Entry"

### Delete Entry
1. Find the entry in the "All Entries" table
2. Click the **Delete** button (trash icon) on the right
3. Confirm deletion
4. Entry removed ✓

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Accurate Tracking
1. **Add entries consistently** - Log posts the same day or shortly after
2. **Use consistent naming** - Keep post titles clear and descriptive
3. **Include all platforms** - Even if cross-posting, log each platform separately
4. **Update metrics regularly** - Check platforms at consistent times to track engagement growth
5. **Add notes** - Note any special campaigns or promotions

### For Better Analysis
1. **Compare periods** - Use date filters to compare performance week-over-week
2. **Platform comparison** - See which platforms drive most engagement
3. **Content type analysis** - Check which post types perform best for your audience
4. **Trend spotting** - Look at trend charts to identify what works
5. **Export for reports** - Share metrics with your team

### Common Workflows

**Weekly Review**
1. Filter to "Last 7 Days"
2. Review KPI cards
3. Check top performing post
4. Note which platforms had best engagement
5. Plan next week's content

**Platform Analysis**
1. Filter to specific platform (e.g., Instagram)
2. Review platform breakdown
3. Check last 30 days data
4. Identify best performing content types
5. Optimize future posts

**Monthly Reporting**
1. Filter to "Last 30 Days"
2. Screenshot or note all KPI numbers
3. Check trend charts
4. Document top performers
5. Share with management

## ❓ FAQ

A: Not yet. Currently manual entry. Meta integration coming in a future update.

**Q: Can I edit past entries?**  
A: Yes! Click the Edit button on any entry to modify it.

**Q: How do I delete an entry?**  
A: Click the Delete button (trash icon) on any entry in the table.

**Q: Can I export data?**  
A: Data is stored in your account. You can screenshot the dashboard or take note of the numbers.

**Q: Who can see my analytics?**  
A: Only you and admins can see your entries (based on permissions).

**Q: What if I made a mistake entering data?**  
A: Click Edit to fix it. Metrics update automatically.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

**Problem: "Social Analytics" button not showing**
- Solution: Log out and log back in, or refresh the page

**Problem: Charts show "No data"**
- Solution: You haven't added any entries yet. Click "Add Entry" to get started!

**Problem: Entry didn't save**
- Solution: Check your internet connection and try again. Look for error message.

**Problem: Data looks wrong**
- Solution: Edit the entry to correct it. Previous data is overwritten.

**Problem: Page is slow**
- Solution: Try filtering to a smaller date range. Large datasets take longer to render.
